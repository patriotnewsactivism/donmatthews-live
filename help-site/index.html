Frequently asked questions

General support information.

Where should I report a bug?
Use the product's in-app support option when available. If you cannot access the product, email don@donmatthews.live and include the product name, page URL, what you expected, and what happened.
What should I include with a technical support request?
Include the exact URL, device/browser, approximate time of the problem, steps to reproduce it, and a screenshot when useful. Do not email passwords, API keys, recovery codes, or other credentials.
Is the AI Counselor a replacement for professional care?
No. It is a conversational AI product, not emergency or professional mental-health care. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services; in the United States, 988 provides crisis support.
Where can I listen to or download Bad Actors?
Visit music.donmatthews.live for the current official music page, streaming links, track documentation, and available downloads.
Where can I find APEX?
The canonical APEX production domain is apex.donmatthews.live.
How quickly will support respond?
Response time depends on the issue and current workload. For outages or account-access problems, put the affected product and “urgent” in the subject line and include enough detail to reproduce the problem.

Send a concise support message and include the affected product.
